# Zhyvo brand foundation

> Status: founder-approved naming foundation, version 0.2, 2026-08-03.
> Visual identity and logo are not yet approved.

## Name

- Brand: **Zhyvo**
- Ukrainian root: **живо**
- Ukrainian pronunciation: **жи́во**
- International pronunciation cue: **ZHYY-voh**
- Always write `Zhyvo`; do not use `ZHYVO`, `ZhyVo`, `Zhyvo App` or translate the brand name.

## Product category

A private, temporary event media-sharing app.

## Primary message

**Подія очима всіх.**

International version: **Every angle of the moment.**

## Launch descriptor

**Фото й відео з події в оригінальній якості — від усіх і в одному місці.**

English: **Private event photos and videos in original quality, all in one place.**

## Value proposition

For friends and guests who want all the photos and videos after an event, Zhyvo is a temporary shared room where everyone uploads original-quality media without registration or messenger compression.

## Voice

- simple and human;
- warm without sentimentality;
- confident without technical jargon;
- concise and action-oriented;
- transparent about privacy and automatic deletion.

## Naming architecture

- Product and Telegram display name: **Zhyvo**
- Production Telegram bot username: `@zhyvoappbot` (reserved 2026-08-03)
- Preferred domain: `zhyvo.app`
- Primary action: **Відкрити Zhyvo** / **Open Zhyvo**
- Shared spaces remain **кімнатами** / **rooms** in product copy.

## Reserved claims

Do not claim “lossless”, “permanent backup”, “unlimited” or “completely secure”. Use the factual claims: original upload quality, private room access, optional PIN/password and automatic deletion after the selected lifetime.
