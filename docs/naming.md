# Naming workshop

> Status: **Zhyvo approved by the founder on 2026-07-31**.
> `@zhyvoappbot` was reserved on 2026-08-03. **PhotoDrop** remains only in internal technical identifiers where renaming would add migration risk without user value.

## 1. Brand core

### What the product really is

A private, temporary place where everyone at an event contributes original photos and videos, so the group gets the whole story without chasing files through chats.

### Core promise

**One event. Every point of view.**

Ukrainian expression: **Одна подія. Усі її моменти.**

### Emotional outcome

The user should feel: “I did not miss the moments happening around me.”

### Brand character

- warm, not sentimental;
- modern and premium, not luxurious;
- social, but not a public social network;
- effortless, not childish;
- private and trustworthy, not corporate cloud storage.

### The enemy

- messenger compression;
- “скинь мені ті фото” for the fifth time;
- media split between different phones and platforms;
- permanent accounts and complicated setup.

## 2. Naming rules

The final name should:

- work for both photos and video;
- be pronounceable in Ukrainian and English;
- fit a Telegram bot, app icon and spoken recommendation;
- feel useful for birthdays, trips, parties and weddings without becoming a wedding-only brand;
- avoid `Photo`, `Pic`, `Event`, `Share`, `Drop`, `Cloud`, `Guest` and `Roll`;
- avoid invented SaaS names with a random `-ly`, `-io` or `-ora` ending;
- have a plausible domain and social-handle path;
- be capable of carrying a story larger than the current MVP.

## 3. Founder taste decoded

The reference brands are **Shopify**, **Feastables** and **Toskovat**. They point to a more specific naming style than the first workshop:

- simple enough to remember after hearing it once;
- a real linguistic root, not a random startup syllable;
- lively and slightly playful rather than sterile-premium;
- capable of carrying a story beyond a literal feature description;
- Ukrainian origin is an advantage when the word remains pronounceable internationally.

This makes **ThisOnce** feel too editorial and grammatically awkward for the intended brand. It is no longer the lead candidate.

## 4. Round two shortlist

This is an initial collision and domain-signal scan dated **2026-07-31**, not legal or trademark clearance.

| Candidate | Idea | Strength | Main risk | Domain signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Zhyvo** | Ukrainian «живо»: vividly, energetically, as if alive | Short, ownable-feeling, Ukrainian character, works beyond one feature | English speakers need to learn the `zh` sound; `.com` is registered | `zhyvo.app` returned no RDAP registration record |
| **Gatherables** | The photos and videos everyone gathers together | Playful, product-relevant, closest to the Feastables naming energy | Long and less effortless to pronounce in Ukrainian | `.com` returned no RDAP record; `.app` check was rate-limited |
| **Uncrushed** | Original-quality media, untouched by messenger compression | Distinctive, rebellious and built around a real user pain | Can also evoke emotions or physical crushing; narrower product story | `uncrushed.app` returned no RDAP record; `.com` is registered |
| **Manytakes** | One event seen through many people's takes | Immediately communicates multiple perspectives | Sounds like a production studio; an active film company owns the `.com` | `manytakes.app` returned no RDAP record |
| **Everytake** | Every person's version of the event | Clear and international | Both primary domains are registered; an existing company uses the name | `.com` and `.app` are registered |

### Recommendation: Zhyvo

Pronunciation: **ZHYY-voh** / «жи́во».

Why it is the strongest fit:

- it has a real meaning and emotional temperature, like the references, without copying their construction;
- it describes how the product should feel, not merely what the upload button does;
- it can cover event rooms today and a broader shared-memory product later;
- it is short enough for an app icon, spoken recommendation and Telegram bot;
- the Ukrainian root gives the international brand a genuine origin story.

Brand idea:

> **Zhyvo**
> Every angle of the moment.

Ukrainian launch line:

> **Zhyvo**
> Подія очима всіх.

Clear product descriptor for the first launch:

> Private event photos and videos in original quality.

The descriptor is important at launch: the name creates recognition; the descriptor explains the product.

### Backup: Gatherables

This is the most playful candidate and the closest match to the Shopify/Feastables instinct. Its idea is strong: every guest contributes something worth gathering. It loses to Zhyvo because the pronunciation is heavier and the word is already common in gaming for collectible resources.

### Wildcard: Uncrushed

This is the most memorable product-truth candidate. It could support sharp communication such as “Your night, uncrushed.” It should remain a wildcard rather than the main name because the brand would become tightly associated with compression, while the product's larger value is collecting everyone's point of view.

## 5. Candidates removed in this round

- **Manytakes** — active video-production company on the exact `.com`.
- **Takeables** — active reusable-cup company and software platform.
- **Alltakes** — highly relevant US trademark application covering digital media transmission and social networking.
- **Fulltake** — active video-editing and sound-design studio.
- **Keepables** — historical photo-printing trademark creates an avoidable category association.
- **Spomyn** — multiple current apps, including memory and journal products.
- **Poruch** — multiple current apps and services.
- **Vulyk** — existing Ukrainian software projects.
- **Blysk** — current app and consumer brands.
- **Svitlyna** — generic in Ukrainian photography and already used by studios.

## 6. Earlier territories (archive)

### A. The once-only moment — recommended territory

The event happens once. Everyone sees a different piece. The product brings those pieces together.

#### ThisOnce

Pronunciation: **this once** / «зис ванc».

Why it works:

- emotionally tied to a one-time occasion, not to storage technology;
- short, distinctive and editorial;
- can grow beyond event galleries;
- supports strong language: “Keep this once”, “Everyone who was there”, “One event. Every side of it.”

Possible positioning:

> **ThisOnce**
> One event. Every side of it.

Ukrainian:

> **ThisOnce**
> Одна подія. Усі її моменти.

Initial availability signal on 2026-07-20:

- no obvious event-photo product with the exact name in a basic web/App Store search;
- `thisonce.app` returned no RDAP registration record;
- `thisonce.com` is registered;
- Telegram pages for `@thisoncebot` and `@thisonceappbot` showed generic contact pages, but only BotFather can confirm that a username is actually available;
- trademark clearance has not been completed.

Risks:

- it is an English phrase rather than an immediately descriptive noun;
- the brand meaning will rely on the tagline during launch;
- native English review is still desirable before final commitment.

### B. The complete perspective

The value is not another album; it is seeing the part of the event captured by everyone else.

#### WholeScene

Possible positioning:

> **WholeScene**
> Everyone caught a different part.

Strengths:

- clearly communicates completeness and multiple viewpoints;
- applies to photo and video;
- `wholescene.app` returned no RDAP registration record on 2026-07-20.

Weaknesses:

- longer and more descriptive than ThisOnce;
- sounds slightly more like a media-production tool;
- `wholescene.com` is registered.

#### Manyside

Idea: every event has many sides.

Strengths: short, ownable-looking, collective.
Weaknesses: coined English grammar feels less natural; it needs more explanation and is not yet strong enough to reserve.

### C. The story told by everyone

The gallery is a collective retelling of what happened.

#### ToldBy

Possible positioning:

> **ToldBy**
> Your day, told by everyone.

Strengths:

- excellent verbal brand system;
- naturally supports weddings, trips, birthdays and community events;
- feels human rather than technical.

Weaknesses:

- both `toldby.com` and `toldby.app` are registered;
- would require a modified domain and deeper trademark work;
- therefore it is a territory reference, not the current reservation candidate.

### D. Ukrainian-rooted togetherness

This direction could create a globally distinctive Ukrainian-founded brand, but the obvious words are already crowded.

- **VKUPI** has excellent meaning (“together”) and both `.com`/`.app` returned no RDAP record, but an active Ukrainian NGO already uses the exact legal name.
- **SPILNO** is heavily used by UNICEF initiatives, Ukrainian organisations and an existing `.app` project.
- **VSI TUT** is already used by Ukrainian community initiatives in Europe.

Conclusion: keep the Ukrainian-rooted territory open, but do not force a weak transliteration merely to make the origin visible.

## 7. Current decision

The previous `SameRoll / GatherRoll / Folio48 / CommonRoll` shortlist is retired. It describes mechanics but does not create a memorable brand.

The founder approved **Zhyvo** on 2026-07-31. The next gate is reserving the domain and Telegram bot handle, followed by a formal trademark screen. **Gatherables** and **Uncrushed** remain archived backups, not active candidates.
